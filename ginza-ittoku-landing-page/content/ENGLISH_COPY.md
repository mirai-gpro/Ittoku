Sengoku period general (1560-1619), famous for his "愛" (Love/Compassion) helmet ornament. While other warriors chose fierce symbols, Kanetsugu believed true strength came from wisdom and compassion. Museum-quality craftsmanship representing one of history's most philosophical samurai.

## FAQ SECTION

### Section Headline:
```
Frequently Asked Questions
```

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
```
We recommend booking at least 7 days in advance, especially for weekends and peak seasons (cherry blossom season March-April, autumn leaves November). Last-minute bookings may be accommodated based on availability - contact us to check.
```

**Q: Is the ¥33,000 per person all-inclusive?**
```
Yes. It includes 2 hours of complete privacy in our space, premium sake & spirits selection, seasonal kaiseki-style multi-course menu, personal cultural guide service, and access to all ¥300M of cultural treasures. Additional premium bottles (like rare KI NO BI gin) or extended time can be arranged for additional cost.
```

**Q: Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?**
```
Absolutely. We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, and specific allergy requirements with advance notice (minimum 3 days before your visit). Please specify all dietary needs when booking so our chefs can prepare accordingly.
```

**Q: Do you speak English and Chinese?**
```
Yes. Our staff includes fluent English and Mandarin Chinese speakers. Our VIP chamber also features multilingual digital guides that explain each cultural treasure in English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), and Japanese. You'll understand everything you're experiencing.
```

**Q: Is photography allowed?**
```
Yes! Photography for personal use is encouraged - we want you to capture memories. We also offer professional photography services for special occasions (additional fee). Flash photography of the artwork is restricted to preserve the delicate pigments and gold leaf.
```

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
```
Cancellations 7 or more days before your reservation: 50% refund
Cancellations within 7 days: No refund (we turn away other bookings for your privacy)
We strongly recommend travel insurance for international visitors in case of flight changes or emergencies.
```

**Q: What should I wear?**
```
Smart casual is perfectly appropriate - think business casual or nice dinner attire. Traditional Japanese clothing (kimono, yukata) is welcome and appreciated. We can arrange kimono rental services if you're interested in wearing traditional attire during your visit.
```

**Q: How do I get there from my hotel?**
```
Ginza Ittoku is at Ginza 8-3-11, easily accessible via Tokyo Metro (5 min from Shimbashi Station, 7 min from Ginza Station). Most hotels can arrange taxi service (¥1,500-3,000 from major hotels). We'll send detailed directions with your booking confirmation.
```

**Q: Can I bring children?**
```
We welcome guests of all ages. However, the experience is designed for cultural appreciation and quiet contemplation, so it may be more suitable for children age 12 and above. The complete privacy of our space means families can enjoy at their own pace without disturbing others.
```

**Q: Do you host private events or corporate functions?**
```
Yes. Our space can be reserved exclusively for corporate entertainment, team building, private celebrations, or special events. We can accommodate 2-8 guests for standard bookings. Contact us for customized packages, extended time options, or special arrangements.
```

**Q: What makes this different from other luxury restaurants in Tokyo?**
```
Ginza Ittoku isn't a restaurant - it's a cultural sanctuary. We invested ¥300 million in authentic imperial palace reconstructions, not modern interior design. You have complete privacy (no other guests), authentic cultural treasures (not replicas for decoration), and a focus on immersive experience rather than just fine dining. This is closer to visiting a private museum than going to dinner.
```

**Q: Can I purchase the cultural items I see?**
```
The cultural treasures (samurai armor, Kano School paintings, gold leaf fusuma) are permanent installations and not for sale - they're part of the space's cultural heritage. However, we can connect you with the artisan workshops (like HORIKIN for gold leaf) if you're interested in commissioning similar pieces for your own collection.
```
