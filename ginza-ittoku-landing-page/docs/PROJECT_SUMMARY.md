## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges:

**Challenge 1: Pricing Perception**
- **Risk:** ¥33,000 seems expensive
- **Mitigation:** Emphasize ¥300M value, exclusivity, once-in-a-lifetime
- **Solution in design:** Show tangible value throughout page

**Challenge 2: Booking Friction**
- **Risk:** 3-day advance requirement, full payment
- **Mitigation:** Clear communication, easy process, travel insurance recommendation
- **Solution in design:** FAQ addresses this, multiple contact methods

**Challenge 3: Competition for Attention**
- **Risk:** Tokyo has thousands of experiences
- **Mitigation:** Unique positioning, SEO, paid ads, hotel partnerships
- **Solution in design:** Strong hero section, clear differentiation

**Challenge 4: Seasonal Variation**
- **Risk:** Slow periods (summer heat, winter cold)
- **Mitigation:** Seasonal events, special promotions
- **Solution in design:** Email signup for event notifications

**Challenge 5: Language Barriers**
- **Risk:** International guests hesitant
- **Mitigation:** Multilingual staff, digital guides
- **Solution in design:** Clearly state English/Chinese support
